Research

Data Quality in WebSocket-Based Real-Time Systems: A Framework for Reliable Data Streaming

International Journal of Versatile Research and Analysis (IJVRA), Volume 4, Issue 10, October 2026. Written with Prof. Rashmi Chavan.

Messages in a live WebSocket stream can go missing, arrive twice, arrive late or arrive out of order. The paper proposes the WebSocket Data Quality Assessment Framework (WDQAF), which scores a stream on dimensions such as completeness, validity, timeliness and ordering, then combines them into one score from 0 to 100.

I tested it by injecting faults into synthetic sensor data across seven scenarios, from a clean stream to a run with several faults at once.
